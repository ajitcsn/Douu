# Douu Google Play store listing

## App name

Douu

## Short description

Gentle reminders to stay close to the people who matter.

## Full description

Douu is a private, local-first way to stay in touch with your people.

Import the contacts you care about, sort them into communities such as Family, Friends, or Work, and choose how often you want to reconnect. Each day, Douu offers a small, flexible quest: message any few people who are due for a hello.

There is no pressure and no social feed. Skip someone when the moment is not right, customise message starters, and celebrate the rhythm you build over time.

Your contacts and reach-out history stay on your device. Douu has no account, no analytics, no ads, and no server. It opens WhatsApp only when you choose to message someone.

Features:

- Sort contacts into your own communities
- Set a different reconnection rhythm for each community
- Get gentle Daily Quests and optional Side Quests
- Open a WhatsApp chat with one tap
- Use editable message starters
- Track your own momentum and community health
- Keep data local, with optional encrypted backups
