Douu Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Douu helps you remember to stay in touch with people. It is designed to work locally on your device.

Information Douu uses

With your permission, Douu reads contacts from your device so you can choose people and organise them into communities. It stores selected contact details, groups, reminder settings, notes, and reach-out history in a local database on your device.

How information is shared

Douu does not operate a server, use analytics, use advertising SDKs, or transmit your contact data. A WhatsApp link opens only when you choose to message someone. Optional backups are created and shared only when you explicitly export them.

Permissions

Contacts access lets you import and organise people. Notification access lets Douu show reminders you choose. You can revoke either permission in Android settings.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it in Douu, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Exported backups are controlled by you.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the support address listed on Douu's Google Play store page.
